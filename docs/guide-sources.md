# What the public guides rest on

Written for whoever changes a guide or the Help center. Every claim about Etsy's rules on the public site rests on a page listed under "Read"; `frontend/lib/marketingSite.test.ts` compares every long quotation in the guides with this file and fails if a guide quotes words that are not here.

## Rules for changing a guide

- A claim about Etsy's rules needs a link, in the same block or in the paragraph leading into its list, to a page that states it.
- A search engine's summary, or a third-party blog, is not a source. The Help Center can be read directly; `www.etsy.com` answers automated requests with 403, so its pages are read by a person.
- Anything else in a guide is labelled as our own advice, and does not say or suggest that Etsy said it.
- A page nobody has read goes under the guide's "Also read on Etsy" list and supports no claim.

## Read

Read directly on 1 November 2026 (HTTP 200) unless a line says otherwise. The Help Center articles show no date of their own.

### How to Create a Listing
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing

- "A listing's title can be up to 140 characters long. Consider using less than 15 words when including the traits that make your item special."
- "You can add up to 20 photos and 2 videos that are 5-15 seconds in length to each listing."
- "Tags: Add up to 13 tags that describe your item."

### How to Use Tags to Get Found in Search
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336307-How-to-Use-Tags-to-Get-Found-in-Search

- "A tag is a word or short phrase that describes your item. Etsy matches tags with shoppers' searches to find relevant results."
- "You can use up to 13 tags per listing."
- "A single tag can be a short descriptive phrase (for example, "silver earrings" or "minimalist jewelry")."
- "Tags are helpful when there's no matching attribute and are great for aspirational or buyer-minded search terms (for example, "gift for her")."
- "To increase your search visibility, use a diverse array of tags."
- "Each tag can contain up to 20 characters. Tags can only include spaces, letters, numbers. You're able to use ' and - within words and phrases, but cannot start your tag with these characters."

### How Do Etsy's Listing Image Requirements Apply to My Listings?
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/44297885802647-How-Do-Etsy-s-Listing-Image-Requirements-Apply-to-My-Listings

- "Etsy requires at least one unedited photo for most items."
- "For other images, you can use photo editing tools, including AI, as long as your images accurately represent what buyers will receive."
- "An unedited photo should show the actual item against a real background and must not be edited or changed in any way."
- "Some exceptions may apply depending on the types of items you sell."
- "Make your first listing photo the clearest, most realistic representation of the item buyers will receive."
- "If a production partner prints your original design on a standard base product (like a t-shirt or mug), you may use a stock photo mockup, if the stock photo's owner has authorized its use."

### How to Open a Second Shop on Etsy
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017604474-How-to-Open-a-Second-Shop-on-Etsy

- "You're welcome to run multiple shops at once on Etsy."
- "A separate Etsy account for each shop"
- "A separate email address on file with each account."
- "If you choose to run multiple shops on Etsy, you must list all the shops you run in the Public Profile section of each account. Each account must also comply with Etsy's Seller Policy."

### What to Do if You Receive a Notice of Intellectual Property Infringement
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034079714-What-to-Do-if-You-Receive-a-Notice-of-Intellectual-Property-Infringement

- "If any of your listings are removed due to an infringement claim, you'll receive an email with the contact information of the party who filed it."
- "We encourage you to directly contact the party that provided the report to Etsy if you have questions about the infringement claim, why your content was identified as allegedly infringing, or how to resolve the issue."
- "Etsy does not make decisions about which listings infringe with intellectual property, and can not speak on behalf of the intellectual property owner."
- "Do not create additional listings for this content unless the issue is resolved with the other party. Repeat reports of infringement may result in the loss of account privileges."

### How to Report Intellectual Property Infringement
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344448-How-to-Report-Intellectual-Property-Infringement

- "In accordance with our Intellectual Property Policy, if the report complies with our policies, we will remove the reported content and contact the affected members."

### How to File a DMCA Counter Notice
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040496854-How-to-File-a-DMCA-Counter-Notice

- "Counter notices can be filed against reports of copyright infringement only."
- "Etsy cannot accept counter notices for non-copyright claims."
- A counter notice includes "a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as the result of a mistake or misidentification."
- "Unless the complaining party notifies Etsy of legal action seeking a court order to restrain you from infringing activity, you may relist the removed material 10 business days after Etsy processes your counter notice."

### How to Set Processing Times, Processing Profiles and "Ship By" Dates
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015588087-How-to-Set-Processing-Times-Processing-Profiles-and-Ship-By-Dates

- "Processing times help set expectations with buyers, and help you reliably ship your orders."
- "You can make changes to your processing schedule at any time."
- "If you edit a processing profile, all of the listings and variations associated with it will update as well."
- "Select which holidays you'll process orders on (US and CA only)."
- "Removing a holiday from your schedule means you're not able to package or prepare orders on that day, and it won't be counted as a processing day."

### Etsy Open API v3, createDraftListing
The published schema, read directly: https://www.etsy.com/openapi/generated/oas/3.0.0.json. The guides link the reference page generated from it, https://developers.etsy.com/documentation/reference/#operation/createDraftListing, which needs a browser to render.

- Title: "valid title strings contain only letters, numbers, punctuation marks, mathematical symbols, whitespace characters, ™, ©, and ®. (regex: /[^\p{L}\p{Nd}\p{P}\p{Sm}\p{Zs}™©®]/u) You can only use the %, :, & and + characters once each."
- Tags: "valid tag strings contain only letters, numbers, whitespace characters, -, ', ™, ©, and ®. (regex: /[^\p{L}\p{Nd}\p{Zs}\-'™©®]/u)"

### Seller Policy, section 1c item 7
https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers (page last updated 5 October 2026). Read by the owner, who gave the wording on 1 November 2026; automated requests get 403.

- It prohibits creating duplicate shops or other actions "for the purpose of shilling, manipulating search or circumventing Etsy's policies".
